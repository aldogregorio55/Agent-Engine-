# The Corniest Jokes Known to Humanity

> Proceed at your own risk. Side effects may include groaning, eye-rolling, and involuntary dad-laughing.

---

## Classic Corn

1. **Why don't scientists trust atoms?**  
   Because they make up everything.

2. **I told my wife she was drawing her eyebrows too high.**  
   She looked surprised.

3. **What do you call a fake noodle?**  
   An impasta.

4. **Why did the scarecrow win an award?**  
   He was outstanding in his field.

5. **I'm reading a book about anti-gravity.**  
   It's impossible to put down.

9. **How do trees access the internet?**
